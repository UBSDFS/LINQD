# Product Vision
**Working name:** LINQD (not finalized; VYBE remains an alternative)

## Product statement
LINQD is a mobile event-discovery and social-planning application that helps people discover local public experiences and organize private plans with their existing circles, without switching between scattered messages, calendars, and RSVP tools.

## Problem
People discover events in one place, coordinate attendance in another, and lose track of who is going. Existing friend groups also need a straightforward way to organize plans privately.

## Product goals
1. Make local event discovery fast and approachable.
2. Give public discovery and private circle plans equal visibility on the home screen.
3. Make hosting, joining, and managing event responses simple.
4. Support guest browsing while providing additional features to authenticated users.
5. Build a maintainable application suitable for iterative real-world launch.

## Non-goals for initial class prototype
Live ticket sales, payments, production authentication, event recommendation algorithms, and full cloud infrastructure.

## Success criteria (to validate, not assumed)
Users can discover an event, understand its details, and submit an RSVP with minimal friction. Track navigation completion, RSVP completion, form errors, and repeat use when analytics and consent are designed.

## Differentiator to test
One home experience for both public weekend discovery and private plans with people you already know.
