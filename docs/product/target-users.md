# Target Users and Research Assumptions
## Primary audiences
1. People seeking nearby public events and weekend activities.
2. Friends and established groups coordinating private plans.
3. Hosts organizing gatherings and tracking responses.

## Assumptions to validate
- People want public discovery and private plans on one home screen.
- Guest browsing reduces signup friction.
- Hosts value a quick view of attendee responses.

## Research plan
Interview 5–8 potential users across discovery, private-circle, and hosting scenarios. Test paper/Figma flows before adding advanced features. Record pain points, not just requested features.
